Delicious Pizza Recipe

Home

Ingredients for recipe:

  • 1 ½ cups warm water (110°F)
  • 2 ¼ teaspoons active dry yeast
  • 3 ½ cups all-purpose flour
  • 2 tablespoons olive oil
  • 2 teaspoons sugar
  • 1 teaspoon salt
  • ½ cup pizza sauce
  • 2 cups shredded mozzarella cheese
  • Your favorite toppings (pepperoni, mushrooms, bell peppers, etc.)

Instructions:

  1. In a small bowl, dissolve the yeast in warm water and let it sit for about 5 minutes until it becomes frothy.
  2. In a large mixing bowl, combine the flour, olive oil, sugar, and salt. Gradually add the yeast mixture and stir until a dough forms.
  3. Knead the dough on a floured surface for about 5-7 minutes until it becomes smooth and elastic.
  4. Place the dough in a lightly oiled bowl, cover it with a damp cloth, and let it rise in a warm place for about 1 hour or until it has doubled in size.
  5. Preheat your oven to 475°F (245°C).
  6. Punch down the risen dough and roll it out on a floured surface to your desired thickness.
  7. Transfer the rolled-out dough to a pizza stone or baking sheet.
  8. Spread the pizza sauce evenly over the dough, leaving a small border around the edges.
  9. Sprinkle the shredded mozzarella cheese over the sauce.
  10. Add your favorite toppings.
  11. Bake the pizza in the preheated oven for about 12-15 minutes, or until the crust is golden and the cheese is bubbly and slightly browned.
  12. Remove the pizza from the oven and let it cool for a few minutes before slicing and serving. Enjoy your homemade pizza!
