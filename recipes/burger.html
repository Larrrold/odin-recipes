Classic Burger Recipe

Home

Ingredients for recipe:

  • 1 lb ground beef (80% lean)
  • 4 hamburger buns
  • 4 slices of cheddar cheese
  • 1 tomato, sliced
  • 1 cup lettuce, shredded
  • 1 small onion, sliced
  • Pickles (optional)
  • Ketchup, mustard, and mayonnaise (optional)
  • Salt and pepper to taste

Instructions:

  1. Preheat your grill or stovetop pan to medium-high heat.
  2. Divide the ground beef into 4 equal portions and shape them into patties. Season both sides with salt and pepper.
  3. Place the patties on the grill or pan and cook for about 4-5 minutes on each side for medium doneness. Adjust the cooking time according to your preference.
  4. During the last minute of cooking, place a slice of cheddar cheese on each patty to melt.
  5. While the patties are cooking, lightly toast the hamburger buns on the grill or in a toaster.
  6. Assemble the burgers by placing the cooked patties on the bottom buns. Top with lettuce, tomato slices, onion slices, and pickles if desired.
  7. Add ketchup, mustard, and mayonnaise to taste.
  8. Place the top buns on the assembled burgers and serve immediately. Enjoy your classic homemade burgers!
